# YouTube Video Analysis Insights

Evidence-based patterns differentiating high-performing from low-performing streams.

---

## Insight 1

**Signal**: Scene Cut Frequency

- High performers: 11.3 ± 6.2 cuts/min
- Low performers: 1.8 ± 0.2 cuts/min
- Difference: +9.5 cuts/min

**Insight**: "High-performing streams introduce a visual change every 5 seconds, while low-performing streams remain static for 34 seconds. Frequent cuts sustain viewer attention and reduce cognitive load during passive consumption."

**Recommendation**: Aim for 1 scene change every 8–10 seconds through cuts, transitions, or on-screen movement.

## Insight 2

**Signal**: Motion Intensity

- High performers: 39.8/100
- Low performers: 23.0/100
- Difference: +16.8/100

**Insight**: "High-performing videos show 40% more on-screen motion and movement compared to low performers. Dynamic visuals signal active content and help maintain viewer engagement."

**Recommendation**: Incorporate camera movement, character motion, or visual effects to increase perceived energy and activity.

## Insight 3

**Signal**: Audio Dynamics (Silence & Loudness Variance)

- High performers: 26.0% silence, 8.1 loudness variance
- Low performers: 32.0% silence, 12.1 loudness variance

**Insight**: "High performers minimize dead-air (26.0% vs 32.0%) and maintain consistent audio energy. Low performers have more silence and less dynamic sound, which can cause viewer drop-off."

**Recommendation**: Fill silence with background music, sound effects, or narration. Vary audio levels naturally to keep the experience engaging.

## Methodology

- **Sample**: 2 high-performing + 2 low-performing videos from Fighting Games niche
- **Signals Analyzed**: Scene cuts, motion intensity, color variance, silence ratio, loudness variance
- **Validation**: Insights based on statistical comparison and manual video review

## Limitations

- Small sample size (4 videos) limits generalizability
- Patterns observed in this niche may not apply to others
- Cannot measure storytelling quality or production value
